A note on LuxRoom / Ho Chi Minh City

Objects with
a softer point
of view.

We select furniture for its ability to make an ordinary day feel more settled: a better angle for reading, a softer landing at night, a table that lets conversation take its time.

The point of view

Good rooms do not ask to be noticed.
They make space for you.

LuxRoom is built around proportion, texture and the way an object becomes part of a daily ritual. We look for quiet forms that live well together rather than a single loud gesture.

01 / Proportion

Useful, then beautiful.

Every piece begins with how it will be used, touched and moved around.

02 / Texture

Material as atmosphere.

Timber, woven textiles and ceramic surfaces bring a room back to the hand.

03 / Longevity

Made for the long view.

We choose forms that can outlast a passing visual mood.

A local rhythm

Designed around real lives, not a perfect scene.

We work with slow mornings, visits that linger and the practical choreography of living closely together. That is why our edit leaves enough room for warmth, wear and your own point of view.
