# LuxRoom Editorial Redesign Brief

LuxRoom will become a **material-led contemporary interiors** brand rather than a Danish lifestyle replica. The site will use a warm mineral base (#F3F0E9), deep charcoal-green ink (#252A24), muted clay accent (#A55B3A), and a typography pairing of **Newsreader** (display) with **DM Sans** (UI/body). This differentiates it from Ferm Living while retaining a high-end editorial sensibility.

The homepage will follow a distinct asymmetric composition: a single panoramic hero, editorial copy block, a three-card material collection grid, a one-column product selection with inline prices, a service/value band, a journal-like consultation split section, a community gallery, and a concise sign-up panel. It will avoid Ferm Living's split 50/50 hero, its five-column category row, its image composition, brand copy, and brand assets.

Core content voice: calm, precise, material-focused, and locally grounded. Each section should describe how a room feels and functions, rather than emulate any other retailer's collection naming or phrasing.
