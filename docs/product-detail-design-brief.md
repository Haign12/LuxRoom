# LuxRoom Product Detail Direction

The existing product detail establishes a strong LuxRoom DNA: three equal visual moments at the top, an editorial serif product title, an expansive information field, a compact black purchase action, a service-trust band, and a material/specification section paired with a warm room image. The redesign will preserve this structure rather than replace it with a generic ecommerce layout.

The upgraded page will align with the updated home system through Newsreader + DM Sans typography, mineral paper background, charcoal-green ink, clay highlights, and a non-pill navigation header. It will retain the rounded gallery only as the user-designed product-detail signature. All other surfaces will remain flat and quiet.

The page will use the product query parameter already supported by the storefront. The selected product name and price will render dynamically. Quantity and add-to-cart will retain the existing cart runtime. Gallery image selection, variant choices, accordions, and add-to-cart feedback will become clearer without adding intrusive UI.
